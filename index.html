I'm a passionate Web Designer and Front-End Developer from Cape Town, South Africa, focused on building digital experiences that look sharp and work beautifully. I build with precision and dedication and focus on delivering quality work on each project.

I'm currently a Web Designer & Front-End Developer Intern at Connect Solutions NPC, while continuing to build my skills and portfolio through real-world work and personal projects.

I design and build modern, clean, secure, responsive and user-friendly websites and e-commerce (online) stores. I have worked with Shopify, WordPress, Divi and Elementor , while using HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Python to bring ideas and functionality to life.
